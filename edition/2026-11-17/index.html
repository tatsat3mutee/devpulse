  • WhatSWE-CoWork benchmarks coding agents on 101 development assignments across real repositories, programming languages, and application domains.
  • HowThe two leading models passed about 63% of fail-to-pass tests but completed fewer than 8% of assignments; supplying full requirements improved partial test results without raising task completion.
  • Why it mattersIts organization simulation tests requirements discovery and delivery, showing that passing some executable tests is not equivalent to completing an engineering assignment.
