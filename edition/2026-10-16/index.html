A scan of 217 million web responses found few prompt-injection headers

The scan found 30 injection attempts among 7,959 sites with AI-targeted headers; in tests, models obeyed 7 of 9 times only when the user prompt gave the site authority. The more actionable risk is sites identifying AI clients and potentially serving them different content.

  • WhatAI-targeted HTTP headers are rare: 30 of 7,959 sites sending AI-directed headers tried to give an agent orders.
  • HowFetch tools differ: some pass headers into model context, while body-only tools drop them; tested Claude Code models obeyed only when users gave sites authority.
  • Why it mattersEngineers should check what their agent’s fetch tools expose and whether sites can identify AI clients by user-agent.

Velox routes video-world attention by spatial memory and speeds chunk 48 denoising 2.43×

Velox uses camera-frustum overlap to select historical context, reporting 4.36× faster attention and 2.43× faster denoising at chunk 48. The approach targets growing KV-cache costs in long interactive video rollouts.

  • What- Velox is a training-free attention router that selects region-level context for chunked video diffusion world models.
  • Method- It represents history as camera frustum slabs and uses recency-priority multiwarp for current-block depth; overlap modulates importance for covered regions.
  • Why it matters- By reducing attention growth with rollout length, it reports 2.43× faster denoising at chunk 48 while staying within 0.15 average points of dense LingBot-Fast-Cam on WBench navigation.
