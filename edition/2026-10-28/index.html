Paper

Top-k channel selection avoids calibration and improves MoE decoding reads

Across 43 comparisons, top-k channel selection matches or beats calibrated thresholds in 40 at the same read budget, and avoids repetition-loop collapse on Gemma 4. The method could simplify memory-bound MoE decoding at small batch sizes.

  • WhatSmall-batch MoE decoding is bottlenecked by loading routed-expert weights; channel selectors predict which channels matter and read only those weights.
  • MethodTop-k selects each token’s k highest-scoring channels without calibration; at 13% and 18% budgets, it matched or beat threshold versions in 40 of 43 comparisons.
  • Why it mattersFor MoE serving, top-k avoids threshold calibration and provides a fixed per-token channel read count; calibrated thresholds did not ensure a known budget or consistent accuracy gains.
