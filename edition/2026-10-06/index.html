Fixed-point training improves looped models and speeds prefill and RL updates

The work uses learned depth priors and orthogonal input injection to improve perplexity across 100M–1.6B models, and reports 1.79× faster student prefill and 2× faster RL updates. Its fixed-point analysis connects model architecture choices to training and inference costs.

  • WhatFixed-point training tunes looped language models to reduce recurrence costs across training, decoding, prefill, and reinforcement learning.
  • MethodA learned depth prior and orthogonal input injection lower perplexity across tested scales; fixed points enable truncated backpropagation and terminal KV sharing.
  • Why it mattersFor model builders, the approach enables KV sharing with almost no accuracy loss, prefill up to 1.79× faster, and RL gradient updates 2× faster.

Tail-Influence Sampling allocates rollouts to improve CVaR estimates

Tail-Influence Sampling estimates which conditional components most affect policy CVaR, then reallocates evaluation queries; on CliffWalking it reduces MSE by 41% versus learned occupancy and 76% versus a comparison method.   • WhatTail-Influence Sampling (TIS) allocates a fixed evaluation budget to estimate a policy’s lower-tail CVaR.
  • MethodTIS estimates tail-influence scales from a pilot model and directs fresh queries to the kernels that matter most to the tail.
  • Why it mattersIt helps engineers evaluate rare-failure risk with costly rollouts instead of relying on mean returns alone.
