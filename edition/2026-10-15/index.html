Paper

KV4-RL protects selected KV positions to speed INT4 rollouts

KV4-RL keeps attention sinks and recent context in INT8 while quantizing the rest to INT4, achieving 1.80× cumulative rollout speedup and 1.27× full-iteration speedup with final accuracy within 0.5 percentage points of BF16. The results show how selective KV-cache protection can improve RL training throughput while limiting quality loss.

  • What- KV4-RL combines calibration-free INT4 KV-cache quantization with selective INT8 protection for attention sinks and recent context.
  • Method- Protecting a bounded number of positions reduces sampler mismatch while retaining most INT4 speedup; INT4 alone can degrade learning even with TIS.
  • Why it matters- In GRPO training, KV4-RL achieved 1.80× cumulative rollout and 1.27× full-iteration speedups over BF16, with final accuracy within 0.5 points on two models.
