LatentGRM cuts reward-judge inference time by up to 7x

LatentGRM compresses evaluation trajectories by 8.9–9.2x and reduces judge inference time by 6.1–7.0x at vote@5, with competitive preference accuracy against explicit SFT judges. It shows how rubric-guided latent reasoning can lower the cost of model evaluation.

  • WhatLatentGRM compresses rubric-guided evaluations into continuous trajectories for reward-judge pairwise judgments, with an interpreter reconstructing evaluation text.
  • MethodAt 8B scale, it compresses evaluation trajectories by 8.9–9.2x and cuts total judge inference time by 6.1–7.0x at vote@5, with competitive preference accuracy.
  • Why it mattersLower inference cost while preserving competitive judgment quality can benefit systems that need to run repeated reward evaluations.
