Progress-aligned recurrence reaches 95.5% accuracy at 16 times training length

PAR aligns each recurrent iteration with the state after the same number of operations and normalizes recurrent states; a Qwen2.5-0.5B model reaches 95.5% accuracy at 512 moves after training on at most 32. The ablations identify which training choices enable length extrapolation.

  • WhatProgress-Aligned Recurrence trains each iteration to predict the state after the corresponding number of operations and anchors recurrent-state norms to the first iteration.
  • MethodOn swap-S_5, a model trained through 32 moves reaches 95.5% full-state accuracy at 512 moves; removing norm anchoring cuts extrapolation below 2.2×.
  • Why it mattersPer-iteration supervision and norm control offer a way to extend recurrent models to sequential problems deeper than those seen in training.
