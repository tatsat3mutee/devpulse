WeVisCut combines shared production state with verified replay to train a single 9B policy on more than 130,000 examples, and reports performance comparable to Claude Opus 5 on its 100-task benchmark. Its harness and data pipeline offer practical lessons for training agents that coordinate tools and recover from failures.

  • WhatWeVisCut is a long-form script-to-video system that trains one 9B policy to coordinate editing and recover from failures.
  • MethodA four-stage pipeline combines editing inversion with verified replay, turning decisions recovered from 3,750 finished videos into over 130K training examples.
  • Why it mattersIts shared production state lets tool feedback inform later decisions and repairs, helping a compact agent manage coherent long-form edits.
