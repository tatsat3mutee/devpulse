Paper

FOAM cuts SOAP optimizer overhead with one-sided preconditioning

FOAM drops one Kronecker factor and replaces SOAP's dense rotated second moment with factored statistics; the paper reports 15–20% K-FAC violation with negligible update impact. This offers a concrete route to lower optimizer memory and eigendecomposition costs in transformer training.

  • WhatFOAM is a lightweight SOAP variant designed to approach AdamW efficiency while retaining SOAP's optimizer benefits.
  • MethodIt uses input-only preconditioning and a factored Adam-moment estimator, removing an eigendecomposition and reducing optimizer-state memory.
  • Why it mattersLower optimizer overhead can make second-order methods more practical for large-model training.
