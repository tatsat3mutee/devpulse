Register recycling cuts NVFP4 attention latency by up to 1.64× on Blackwell

The kernel splits score tiles into reusable register slots, reducing Tensor Core idle time; latency improves up to 1.64×, with end-to-end inference reductions up to 15.34% across four workloads. Its integration into FlashInfer makes the measured optimization relevant to serving engineers.

  • WhatRegister recycling optimizes NVFP4 attention on NVIDIA RTX Blackwell GPUs by reusing score registers during kernel execution.
  • HowAn optimized SageAttention3 kernel splits each N128 score tile into two N64 register slots and reuses them after softmax quantization to overlap work.
  • Why it mattersThe schedule reduces Tensor Core idle time without a second full score buffer; the kernel cut attention latency by up to 1.64× on NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell.
