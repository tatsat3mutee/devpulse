LittleBit-2 uses latent geometry alignment for sub-1-bit LLM compression

LittleBit factorizes weight matrices into binary latent factors with learned scales; LittleBit-2 adds latent rotation and iterative quantization before quantization-aware training. The released implementation gives inference engineers a concrete extreme-compression approach to evaluate.

  • What- LittleBit compresses LLMs below 1 bit per weight using factorized, binarized weight matrices and learned scales.
  • Method- LittleBit-2 aligns SVD-derived latent factors to the binary hypercube with Joint-ITQ before quantization-aware training.
  • Why it matters- Initialization-only changes let engineers target extreme compression without adding inference overhead or changing the deployed factorized layer.
