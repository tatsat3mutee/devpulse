Paper

AltRA-Test evaluates LLM annotators when human panels differ by item

AltRA-Test bootstraps item samples and compares agreement in all-human panels with panels where an LLM replaces one annotator; the authors establish asymptotic consistency and test it in simulations and real data. It gives teams a statistical method for evaluating LLM annotators without shared annotators across items.

  • WhatAltRA-Test assesses whether an LLM can replace human annotators when different items have different subsets of annotators.
  • MethodIt bootstraps items, draws k human annotations per item, replaces one with an LLM annotation, then compares agreement in mixed and all-human panels.
  • Why it mattersThe test gives teams a statistical way to assess LLM judge replacement without requiring every item to share the same human annotators.

Ruby's ruby2shell is a heap overflow; Cloudflare adds WAF rules

Ruby's critical string-case heap overflow can enable unauthenticated RCE in default Rails and classic Sinatra configurations; fixes are available in Ruby 3.3.14, 3.4.13, and 4.0.10. Cloudflare's WAF is only a compensating control, so affected deployments still need patching.

  • What- ruby2shell is a heap buffer overflow in Ruby’s string case conversion; Ruby rates the flaw Critical.
  • How- In 14 legacy encodings, Ruby can write the two-byte uppercase form of ß while checking room for only one byte.
  • Why it matters- Cloudflare WAF rules add a mitigation, but upgrading Ruby is the only complete fix; default Rails and Sinatra configurations can be exposed before authentication.
