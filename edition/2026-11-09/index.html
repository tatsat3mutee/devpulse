  • Why it mattersRunning large sparse models in RAM can avoid GPU requirements, but engineers must account for tight memory budgets and CPU inference performance.

Flink tracks Kafka work with splits rather than committed consumer offsets

  • What- Flink’s Kafka connector tracks work through partition splits and offsets stored in Flink state, rather than relying on Kafka’s committed offsets for processing.
