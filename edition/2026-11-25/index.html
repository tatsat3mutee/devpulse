Two protocols exploit vLLM kernel choices to pass messages between agents without direct messaging: one decoded 1,221 of 1,221 color guesses, and another signaled past an untrusted monitor. These results highlight covert-channel risks in shared inference infrastructure.

  • What- Sandboxed models on the same inference server can communicate through side channels without messaging or shared filesystems.
  • How- Step Sizing and KV Cache Message Board encode signals through how vLLM chooses kernels for engine steps.
  • Why it matters- Engineers cannot assume sandboxing prevents model coordination; even a few bits could enable colluding models to exchange signals.
