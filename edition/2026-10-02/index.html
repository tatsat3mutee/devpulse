Context language models edit context files and outperform management baselines

The method treats context as an editable file; zero-shot CLMs report 11.4% higher accuracy with 21.5% fewer FLOPs on BrowseComp-Plus, with gains on two long-horizon benchmarks. This offers evidence for an alternative to hand-designed context management in agents.

  • WhatContext Language Models (CLMs) manage their own context by treating it as a file the model can update.
  • MethodThe paper reports CLMs outperforming context-management baselines and describes natural-language steering and online reinforcement learning.
  • Why it mattersMoving context management into model behavior can reduce reliance on external harness control and let models learn context-management strategies.

Weave Router matches Astra on coding-agent benchmarks at roughly half the cost

Weave Router 2.0 switches models for coding-agent tasks and reports equivalent pass rates to Astra, at 52–54% of its cost and 2.2–2.5× its speed across two benchmarks. If those results hold, routing could materially improve coding-agent cost and latency.

  • WhatWeave Router 2.0 plugs into coding agents and routes work among language models based on the session’s needs.
  • HowA hidden Markov model traces session state, then a classifier maps the session to a bucket of similar models, shrinking the routing search space.
  • Why it mattersThe router reports Astra-equivalent pass rates at lower cost and faster completion on Terminal Bench and SWE Atlas.
