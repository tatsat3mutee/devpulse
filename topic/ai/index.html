A rank-8 LoRA raises Qwen3-8B accuracy from 15.5% to 99% on 24-line chains

A task-trained rank-8 LoRA at one early layer raises Qwen3-8B exact accuracy from 15.5% to 99% on 24-line chains, with model weights frozen; code and a demo are available. The result shows how a small targeted edit can unlock computation that pretrained models fail to use by default.

  • WhatA task-trained rank-8 LoRA at one early layer extends pretrained transformers' ability to follow references across long chains.
  • MethodWith model weights frozen, Qwen3-8B exact accuracy on 24-line chains rises from 15.5% to 99%; the LoRA relays chain identity through middle layers.
  • Why it mattersDefault answers can understate the computation accessible through a small task-specific edit.

Omni-Embed-Mini adds modalities without changing text embedding weights

The 0.9B-parameter model aligns text, speech, audio, images, video and documents while keeping text weights bit-identical; it reports 49.57 nDCG@10 on MTEB-v2 BEIR-8. The distillation recipe offers a practical route to multimodal retrieval without regressing the text encoder through training.
