WanPE improves long-form video prompts with shot-level cinematic plans

The 397B model uses plans built from 1.05M videos and improves human preference by 10.66–18.84 points at 5–15 seconds and 50.86 points at 30 seconds. It is a substantial example of how prompt planning can improve a generative model’s output.

  • WhatWanPE is a 397B-parameter prompt-enhancement model trained on 1.05M real-world videos to create shot-level cinematic plans.
  • HowIt uses video-grounded reverse construction and Semantic-Consistency GRPO to preserve user requirements across shots and over time.
  • Why it mattersWith Wan3.0, it raised human preference over raw prompts by 10.66–18.84 points at 5–15 seconds and 50.86 points at 30 seconds.

RecCAR aligns cross-modal attention to improve joint video generation

RecCAR regularizes weaker modality-to-video correspondence toward video-to-modality attention, raising Human Anatomy from 0.69 to 0.75 and reducing audio-video desynchronization from 0.804 to 0.752. The method offers a concrete training intervention for joint video, motion, and audio models.
