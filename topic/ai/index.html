Release

Kimi K3.1 Pro adds a million-token context and a faster serving tier

The 3T-class open-weight model is announced with a 1-million-token context, while its Highspeed tier is advertised at 2–3× speed; the team describes stack-wide work on RL, inference, and rollout scheduling. Its training and serving choices are relevant to teams evaluating large-model systems, though weights are not due until Nov. 4.

  • What• Kimi K3.1 Pro is a 3T-class open-weight model with a 1-million-token context; K3.1 Pro Highspeed offers 2–3× its speed.
  • How• The post-trained K3 version uses larger post-training compute and an optimized stack for reinforcement learning across hundreds of downstream domains.
  • Why it matters• Its long context and faster serving tier target responsive, multi-step agent work, including coding and tool use.
