Unpatched Linux ksmbd RCE details leaked to public kernel lists

Public posts exposed details of two unpatched ksmbd remote-code-execution bugs, including their prerequisites: an RDMA device and writable share for one, a guest share for the other. Kernel and SMB operators can use the details to assess exposure while fixes are pending.

  • What- Technical details for unpatched Linux kernel vulnerabilities were leaked to public kernel mailing lists before fixes shipped.
  • How- Two ksmbd remote-code-execution bugs require specific conditions: one needs an RDMA device and writable share; the other needs a guest share.
  • Why it matters- Only systems running ksmbd are exposed to these RCEs; the excerpt reports no fixes in mainline or stable releases and no exploitation in the wild.
