Cloudflare Traces follows requests across platform operations and origins

Cloudflare Traces adds request timelines across supported security rules, transformations, cache decisions, routing, Workers, and origin handling, with sampling controls and OTLP export. Engineers can trace failures across platform boundaries without relying on a single service’s telemetry.

  • WhatCloudflare Traces adds request-level tracing across supported Cloudflare operations and services beyond Workers.
  • HowEach supported step becomes a span with timing, outcome, and attributes; Trace Rules can override baseline sampling.
  • Why it mattersEngineers can follow requests across platform operations and origins, investigate bottlenecks, and export spans via OTLP.

Quick Tunnels can restrict access with accountless email verification

Starting with cloudflared 2026.9.3, Quick Tunnels can use --allowed-mail to limit visitors to chosen addresses or domains, verified by a one-time PIN without requiring either side to have a Cloudflare account. This adds a practical access-control option for sharing local development services, including agent-built projects.

GKE CPU startup boost raises pod CPU during initialization without restarting containers

The preview feature uses the Vertical Pod Autoscaler to increase CPU during container startup and return allocation to its baseline after readiness, without a restart. It targets cold-start delays and the cost of sizing requests for brief CPU peaks.

Cloudflare adds an OHTTP gateway to hide client IPs from app backends

OHTTP sends requests through a relay and gateway so app backends do not see client IP addresses; Cloudflare is offering a managed gateway as a paid add-on. Engineers designing privacy-sensitive services can assess a concrete deployment option.
