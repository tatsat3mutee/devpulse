A proof-difficulty metric steers LLMs toward less-overlapping mathematical theorems

The work trains a 27B model to predict proof difficulty and reports reducing substantial or full overlap with Mathlib from 91.9% to 30.6%. Its metric offers a concrete approach to selecting outputs for generative research systems.

  • WhatProof difficulty conditioned on premises helps rank theorem interestingness, defined as proof length divided by statement length.
  • HowA trained 27B model predicts proof difficulty; optimizing the metric reduced substantial or full Mathlib overlap from 91.9% to 30.6%.
  • Why it mattersQuantifiable ranking signals can guide conjecture selection and proof search in formal mathematical libraries.
