T²Quant allocates diffusion-model activation precision by task and timestep

T²Quant uses a label-free sensitivity signal to assign activation precision across denoising steps, improving existing quantization methods by up to 6.7 percentage points at the same bit-width budget. This offers a practical path to better accuracy under fixed inference-precision constraints.

  • What• T²Quant is a mixed-precision quantization framework that allocates diffusion-language-model activation precision by task and denoising timestep.
  • Method• It predicts sensitivity without ground-truth labels and uses that signal to allocate precision across denoising steps under a fixed bit-width budget.
  • Why it matters• Across three DLMs and six benchmarks, it improves existing methods’ average accuracy by up to 6.7 percentage points under the same budget.

A learned unmasking policy improves diffusion language model accuracy and throughput

On LLaDA-8B-Instruct, an 856K-parameter policy using confidence and hidden states achieves the best accuracy–NFE frontier and highest GSM8K throughput; about a 10% NFE reduction offsets policy overhead in tested settings.
