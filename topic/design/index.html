rqlite has replicated SQLite writes with Raft since 2014

rqlite put a Raft log in front of SQLite so writes could be replicated across a cluster, and its creator traces the project from its 2014 start. The history offers a firsthand view of the trade-offs and evolution of distributed SQLite.

  • What• rqlite combines SQLite with a Raft log to replicate writes across a cluster.
  • How• Writes go through the leader and require majority acknowledgment; a partitioned minority refuses writes, making rqlite a CP system.
  • Why it matters• Consensus replication lets a system keep data available across node loss without giving up relational storage.
