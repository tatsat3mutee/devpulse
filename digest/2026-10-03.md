- **[LoopCD improves looped-transformer scores while cutting recurrent compute](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.02185)** (huggingface.co)
  Training-free LoopCD raises Ouro-2.6B-Thinking AIME 2024 pass@1 from 61.88% to 73.33% and cuts forward FLOPs by 22.5–48.2% when fewer loops suffice. It turns intermediate recurrent predictions into a decoding signal that can improve quality and reduce inference compute.
  - LoopCD is a training-free contrastive decoding framework for looped Transformers.
  - It contrasts the final prediction with an earlier recurrent pass, using logits or hidden states to guide token selection.
  - It can halve recurrent loops while matching or exceeding full-depth unguided baselines, cutting forward FLOPs by 22.5%–48.2%.
