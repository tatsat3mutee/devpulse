  - Small-batch MoE decoding is bottlenecked by loading routed-expert weights; channel selectors predict which channels matter and read only those weights.

  - For MoE serving, top-k avoids threshold calibration and provides a fixed per-token channel read count; calibrated thresholds did not ensure a known budget or consistent accuracy gains.
