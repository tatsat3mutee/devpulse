Coding agents score up to 0.923 on a 500-task visual-reasoning benchmark

Three coding agents render answers as programs and score up to 0.923 on a 500-instance benchmark using the same inputs and evaluator as video models. The comparison tests whether code-based agents can outperform a visual-generation route on structured reasoning tasks.

  • WhatCoding agents render visual-task answers as programs, providing a symbolic alternative to video models under identical inputs and evaluation.
  • MethodOn 500 VBVR-Pro-Bench cases, three closed agents scored 0.893–0.923, over 0.22 above the strongest video model; the best open model scored 0.572.
  • Why it mattersFor visual-reasoning systems, executable programs can outperform video generation on tasks with symbolic ground truth.
