WorldDraft finds a trade-off between preserving video structure and matching appearance

Across 432 paired cases, WorldDraft finds that richer draft scaffolding preserves structure but can hurt appearance fidelity, while conflicting cues cause source leakage up to 64%. The benchmark makes competing requirements for video-generation systems measurable.

  • MethodAcross 12 models, richer drafts preserve structure but can hurt appearance fidelity; conflicting cues cause source leakage, reaching 64%.
  • Why it mattersEngineers evaluating video systems must account for the tension between preserving world dynamics and consistently matching visual attributes.
