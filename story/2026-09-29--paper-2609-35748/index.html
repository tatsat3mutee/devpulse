TaH2 directs extra transformer loops to tokens that benefit from more computation

huggingface.co by Yichen You et al. Editor score 7/10

TaH2 post-trains an iteration decider with lookahead depth supervision, allocating extra loops selectively; the paper reports improved test-time scaling on challenging AIME benchmarks. Selective computation could help engineers trade inference cost against reasoning accuracy.

What
TaH2 is an adaptive looped-transformer method that directs extra computation to tokens that benefit from more iterations.
Method
It jointly post-trains the backbone and an iteration decider using lookahead depth supervision to identify when further iteration helps.
Why it matters
On AIME, TaH2 improved the accuracy-compute slope over the non-looped baseline and exceeded its peak accuracy at matched test-time compute.
